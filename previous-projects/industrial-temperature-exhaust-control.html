PLC Temperature & Exhaust Control

A custom industrial automation system for temperature supervision, exhaust process control, operator visibility, and equipment coordination.

Project Overview

Reliable control for an industrial exhaust process

The project required a dependable control system that could monitor critical temperatures, coordinate exhaust fans, and interface with downstream process equipment. Operators also needed a clear way to view system status and control the process.

The completed solution combines PLC logic, an operator interface, electrical protection, field I/O, and equipment interlocks in a purpose-built control cabinet.

Control approach

The system brings monitoring, decisions, and equipment response into one coordinated automation workflow.

Monitor

Read temperature and process signals, display operating status, and make key conditions visible to the operator.

Control

Use Mitsubishi MELSEC-F PLC logic to manage operating modes, temperature protection, timing, and process interlocks.

Respond

Coordinate fans and connected process equipment while providing clear operator controls and alarm indication.

Technical Delivery

Integrated PLC control cabinet

The cabinet was assembled around a Mitsubishi FX3G-40M PLC and supporting I/O. Its electrical layout brings control, protection, switching, and field termination together for practical operation and maintenance.

  • Mitsubishi MELSEC-F PLC control logic
  • HMI and supervisory status display
  • Temperature monitoring and protective control
  • Exhaust fan and downstream equipment interlocks
  • Electrical protection, relays, contactors, and labelled field wiring
  • Structured control cabinet assembly and I/O integration

Inside the system

Control components and field connections are arranged to support clear operation, fault finding, and future servicing.
